The problems, one ticket each

  • The shared parser reads day-first dates month first: issue #1. The parser row above, counted as the desk counts it: the reports footnoted under it stay there, where they are filed, and also have the one-day ticket. Every tenant on file writes the day first; the parser reads the month first, so day and month swap: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.”
  • The booking-v3 form hints MM/DD/YYYY, then reads the date day first: issue #2. The hint row above. The table marks it “no ticket of its own”; it has one now. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Fixing the parser does not change the hint.
  • Bookings move by one day, not by a month: issue #3. The reports in the footnote under the table. “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” A parser that reads the month first swaps day and month; it cannot move a date by one day. Most likely a time-zone conversion: one report has the same meeting on Tuesday in Lisbon and on Monday in Toronto.

How a report is placed

By the desk’s own rules, the ones the table above runs (causes.ts), plus the desk’s own notes (desk-notes.json):

  • the tenant decides the cause, not the reporter’s browser. Intake’s tag is a guess made from the browser locale; the “tagged as” column shows how often it guessed the other cause;
  • a report from a tenant on booking-v3 that came in through the form is hint; every other report from a day-first tenant is parser;
  • a report the desk read as a one-day move stays counted where it is filed, and is footnoted there. It is not a month flip, so it also has the one-day ticket.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. All of these problems arrive under the same subjects: “Wrong day on the invite” and “Month and day swapped?” each carry reports of every problem above. Matching on the subject cannot keep them apart. The explorer on triage shows the same thing: the same subject is not the same cause.

What changes for the desk

  • Ship the parser fix against its own ticket. The hint and one-day tickets stay open after it ships.
  • Send new reports from a booking-v3 form, and reports of a one-day move, to their own tickets, not to #4471.
  • Decision 0001 needs a successor record that places reports by tenant, form and channel, not by subject. That is a new record, signed by its deciders; 0001 stays as it is.
